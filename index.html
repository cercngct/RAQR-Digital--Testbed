Equations used

Every quantity on the other tabs is computed from these relations.

Lindblad master equation

\[ \frac{d\rho}{dt} = -i[H,\rho] - \tfrac12\{\Gamma,\rho\} + \Lambda \qquad (\hbar=1,\ H\ \text{in rad/s}) \]

Rotating-frame Hamiltonian

\[ H=\begin{pmatrix}0&\frac{\Omega_p}{2}&0&0\\ \frac{\Omega_p}{2}&\Delta_p&\frac{\Omega_c}{2}&0\\ 0&\frac{\Omega_c}{2}&\Delta_p+\Delta_c&\frac{\Omega_{RF}}{2}\\ 0&0&\frac{\Omega_{RF}^{*}}{2}&\Delta_p+\Delta_c+\Delta_l\end{pmatrix},\qquad \Delta_j=\omega_{\mathrm{res},j}-\omega_j \]

Δ is the atomic resonance frequency minus the field frequency.

Relaxation and repopulation

\[ \Gamma=\mathrm{diag}\{\gamma,\ \gamma+\gamma_2,\ \gamma+\gamma_3+\gamma_c,\ \gamma+\gamma_4\},\qquad \Lambda=\mathrm{diag}\{\gamma+\gamma_2\rho_{22}+\gamma_4\rho_{44},\ \gamma_3\rho_{33},\ 0,\ 0\} \]

γ is the transit rate (atoms leave at γ and fresh ground-state atoms enter at γ); γc removes |3⟩ population from the four-level subspace. With γ = 0 the trace condition Tr ρ = 1 closes the system.

Rabi frequencies and Gaussian-beam power

\[ \Omega_{ij}=\frac{\mu_{ij}E}{\hbar},\qquad P=\frac{\pi c\varepsilon_0}{8\ln2}F^2|E|^2=\frac{\pi w^2 c\varepsilon_0}{4}|E|^2,\qquad w=\frac{F}{\sqrt{2\ln2}} \]

E is the on-axis peak field; F is the intensity FWHM and w the 1/e² radius.

Doppler shifts and velocity average

\[ \Delta_p'=\Delta_p+k_pv,\quad \Delta_c'=\Delta_c\mp k_cv,\quad \bar\rho_{21}=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\rho_{21}(v)\,\frac{e^{-v^2/u^2}}{u\sqrt\pi}\,dv,\quad u=\sqrt{\frac{2k_BT}{m}} \]

Upper sign: counter-propagating coupling beam. The integral is evaluated with adaptive Gauss–Kronrod (7,15) quadrature over ±6u, seeded with the one-, two- and three-photon resonance velocities; the RF wave vector is perpendicular to the beams.

Susceptibility

\[ \chi=-\frac{2N_0\mu_{12}^2}{\varepsilon_0\hbar\,\Omega_p}\,\bar\rho_{21} \]

Probe amplitude and phase

\[ U_p=U_0\,e^{-\frac{\pi d}{\lambda_p}\Im\chi},\qquad P_1=P_0\,e^{-k_pd\,\Im\chi},\qquad \phi_p=\phi_0+\frac{\pi d}{\lambda_p}\Re\chi \]

z-resolved propagation

\[ \frac{d\Omega_p}{dz}=-\frac{k_p}{2}\,\Im\chi(\Omega_p)\,\Omega_p,\qquad \frac{d\phi_p}{dz}=\frac{k_p}{2}\,\Re\chi(\Omega_p) \]

Beer–Lambert is the special case χ independent of Ωp. The app tabulates χ(Ωp) and ∂χ/∂ΩRF on a log grid, interpolates with a cubic spline and integrates with RK4, including the linearized slope equation.

LO + signal

\[ \Omega_{RF}(t)=\Omega_l+\Omega_x e^{i(2\pi f_\delta t+\theta_\delta)},\qquad |\Omega_{RF}|=\sqrt{\Omega_l^2+\Omega_x^2+2\Omega_l\Omega_x\cos(2\pi f_\delta t+\theta_\delta)}\approx\Omega_l+\Omega_x\cos(2\pi f_\delta t+\theta_\delta) \]

Exact periodic steady state (Floquet)

\[ \rho(t)=\sum_{n=-K}^{K}\rho^{(n)}e^{in\omega t},\qquad (\mathcal L_0-in\omega)\rho^{(n)}+\mathcal L_V\rho^{(n-1)}+\mathcal L_{V^\dagger}\rho^{(n+1)}=-s\,\delta_{n0} \]

ω = 2πfδ, V = (Ωxeiθ/2)|3⟩⟨4|, 𝓛Vρ = −i[V, ρ], s = γ|1⟩⟨1|. Solved by block-tridiagonal elimination for every velocity class; verified against direct time integration of the master equation.

Exact receiver response: z-resolved probe with atomic dynamics

\[ \Omega_p(z,t)=e^{i\phi(z)}\big[r(z)+a(z)e^{i\omega t}+b(z)e^{-i\omega t}\big],\qquad \frac{\partial\Omega_p}{\partial z}=-i\eta\,\bar\rho_{21},\quad \eta=\frac{k_pN_0\mu_{12}^2}{\varepsilon_0\hbar} \] \[ \frac{da}{dz}=-i\phi' a-i\eta\big(R_a^{+}a+R_b^{+}b^*+R_x^{+}\Omega_xe^{i\theta}\big),\qquad \frac{db^*}{dz}=i\phi' b^*+i\eta\big(R_a^{-*}a+R_b^{-*}b^*+R_x^{-*}\Omega_xe^{i\theta}\big) \] \[ \kappa_1=\frac{\mu_{34}}{\hbar}\frac{|a+b^*|}{r\,\Omega_x},\qquad \kappa_2=\frac{\mu_{34}}{\hbar}\frac{|a|+|b^*|}{r\,\Omega_x}\quad\text{at }z=d \]

The R coefficients are the Doppler-averaged first-order responses of ρ21 at ±ω to the signal and to the probe modulation created upstream, obtained from (𝓛0 ∓ iω)ρ(±1) = −𝓛Vρ(0). They are tabulated adaptively in ln Ωp and the equations are integrated with RK4. Verified against an independent slice-by-slice Floquet propagation (agreement better than 10⁻⁴).

First-order (small-signal) response, Beer–Lambert

\[ \rho^{(\pm1)}=-(\mathcal L_0\mp i\omega)^{-1}\mathcal L_{V,V^\dagger}\,\rho^{(0)},\qquad \kappa_1(f_\delta)=\frac{\pi d\,\mu_{34}}{\lambda_p\hbar}\,\big|c_{+}-c_{-}^{*}\big|,\quad c_\pm=\chi^{(\pm1)}/\Omega_x \]

At fδ → 0 this reduces to the quasi-static κ1.

Direct detection, DIOD

\[ V^{(D)}\approx\sqrt G\,\alpha\,\mathcal P_1(\Omega_l)\big[1-2\kappa_1\cos\varphi_1\,U_x\cos(2\pi f_\delta t+\theta_\delta)\big],\qquad \kappa_1=\frac{\pi d\mu_{34}}{\lambda_p\hbar}\big|\Im\chi'(\Omega_l)\big|,\qquad \alpha=\frac{\eta_1q}{2\pi\hbar f_p} \]

Balanced coherent detection, BCOD

\[ V^{(B)}=2\sqrt G\alpha\sqrt{\mathcal P_l\mathcal P_1}\cos(\phi_l-\phi_p),\quad \kappa_2=\frac{\pi d\mu_{34}}{\lambda_p\hbar}|\chi'(\Omega_l)|,\quad \varphi_2=\phi_l-\phi_p+\psi_p,\quad \psi_p=\operatorname{atan2}(\Re\chi',\Im\chi') \]

ψp is taken quadrant-correct with atan2.

Gain

\[ \varrho=4Z_0\alpha^2G\begin{cases}\mathcal P_1^2\kappa_1^2 & \text{DIOD}\\ \mathcal P_l\mathcal P_1\kappa_2^2 & \text{BCOD}\end{cases} \]

Noise

\[ \frac{U_{x,\mathrm{SQL}}}{\sqrt{\mathrm{Hz}}}=\frac{\hbar}{\mu_{34}\sqrt{NT_2}},\ N=\Upsilon N_0\pi w^2d,\quad N_{\mathrm{PSN}}^{(D)}=2qB\bar I_{ph}G,\ \ N_{\mathrm{PSN}}^{(B)}=2qB\alpha(\mathcal P_l+\mathcal P_1)G,\quad N_{\mathrm{ITN}}=k_BTBG \]

SNR and sensitivity

\[ \mathrm{S/N}=\frac{\overline{\tilde V^2}}{N_{\mathrm{QPN}}+N_{\mathrm{PSN}}+N_{\mathrm{ITN}}},\qquad \mathrm{SNR}_{\sigma^2=N/2}=\frac{|\sqrt\varrho\Phi h|^2\mathcal P_s}{\sigma_w^2},\ \ \sigma_w^2=\frac{N_{\mathrm{QPN}}+N_{\mathrm{PSN}}+N_{\mathrm{ITN}}}{2} \]

The default reports S/N of the real IF tone (mean-square signal over noise power in B), the same definition the classical receiver uses. The alternative convention divides the noise power by two (σw² = N/2), which raises the photon-shot and thermal-noise SNR by 3.01 dB; select it under “SNR definition”. Sensitivity is the field amplitude that gives SNR = 1 in 1 Hz; sensitivities add in quadrature.

Classical antenna receiver

\[ \mathrm{SNR}_0=\frac{\eta_0A_{\mathrm{iso}}G_{\mathrm{ant}}\,|U_x|^2/(2Z_0)}{k_BT_{BG}BF},\qquad A_{\mathrm{iso}}=\frac{\lambda^2}{4\pi},\qquad \frac{U_{x,0}}{\sqrt{\mathrm{Hz}}}=\sqrt{\frac{2Z_0k_BT_{BG}F}{\eta_0A_{\mathrm{iso}}G_{\mathrm{ant}}}} \]

Autler–Townes splitting

\[ \Delta f_{AT}=s\,\frac{\Omega_{RF}}{2\pi},\qquad |E|=\frac{2\pi\hbar}{\mu_{34}}\frac{\Delta f_{AT}}{s},\qquad s=\begin{cases}\lambda_c/\lambda_p&\text{probe scanned, thermal, counter-propagating}\\ 1&\text{coupling scanned, or atoms at rest}\end{cases} \]

Transit rate and vapor density

\[ \gamma=\sqrt{\frac{8k_BT}{\pi m}}\,\frac{1}{w\sqrt{2\ln2}},\qquad \log_{10}P_v=\begin{cases}-219.48200+\frac{1088.676}{T}-0.08336185\,T+94.88752\log_{10}T & T<301.59\ \mathrm K\\ 8.22127-\frac{4006.048}{T}-0.00060194\,T-0.19623\log_{10}T & \text{liquid}\end{cases},\quad N_0=\frac{P_v}{k_BT} \]

Pv in torr, T in kelvin.

What the model contains, and what it does not

  • Solved exactly (no weak-probe, no quasi-static, no adiabatic elimination): the 16-element density matrix in steady state for every velocity class, its analytic derivative with respect to ΩRF, the Doppler integral, the Floquet periodic state of the LO–signal beat and the probe propagation along z.
  • Physical approximations that remain: electric-dipole and rotating-wave approximations; hyperfine and Zeeman structure folded into effective dipole moments μij; decay channels as written in Γ and Λ; periodic steady state (switch-on transients are not modelled).
  • Geometry: one-dimensional Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution along the beams; RF wave vector perpendicular to the beams; Rabi frequencies from the on-axis peak intensity (the transverse Gaussian profile is not integrated); uniform RF field in the cell.
  • The receiver model used for the headline numbers (κ, SNR, sensitivity, sweeps, bands, bandwidth) is the exact small-signal model: z-resolved propagation of the modulated probe plus first-order atomic dynamics at fδ. It is first order in Ux; the large-signal Floquet waveform on the Atomic mixer tab is all orders in Ux but uses Beer–Lambert with the input Ωp. The simpler models remain selectable, and the Noise and SNR tab compares all four side by side.
